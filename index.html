Our Story

이야기Hanguk Haven Restaurant & Cafe

달빛 사찰

한국의 맛, 마탈레의 심장

— The heart of Korea, in the hills of Matale —

Hanguk Haven (한국 안식처) was born from a simple wish — to carry the quiet, meditative beauty of Korean dining to the heart of Matale. Every bowl, every leaf of tea, and every plate arrives from the same discipline kept for a thousand years: patience, simplicity, and reverence for the ingredient.

Our kitchen is fire and stone. Our tea room is silence and steam. The recipes are heirlooms, carried from Seoul’s vibrant kitchens — untouched by hurry, unfaithful to nothing.
